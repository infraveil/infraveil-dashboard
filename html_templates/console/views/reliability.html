How checks, budgets and notifications work

Checks come from one Infraveil-hosted location, not a multi-region monitoring network. Fleet selection does not change this workspace-wide scope. An observer outage produces gaps.

A good check meets your expected HTTP status and response-header latency limit. Error budgets use scheduled check slots, not customer traffic or a duration-based uptime SLA. Missing, interrupted and late checks stay unknown. At least ten mature slots are needed for a budget verdict.

Budgets are advisory: they never pause a release or stop an application. For deduplicated failure/recovery notifications, subscribe to Runtime signals in an enabled Edge Integration. This is not on-call paging.

Samples expire after 30 days. Saving changes starts a new series; deleting a monitor removes its check history, not any application data.
